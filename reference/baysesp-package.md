# baysesp: Bayesian Accuracy of Multiple Diagnostic Tests under Partial Verification

Bayesian estimation of the sensitivity, specificity and predictive
values of one or more binary diagnostic tests performed on the same
subjects, when the disease status is ascertained by the reference
standard only for a subset of subjects (partial verification). Supports
joint modelling of K tests, accuracy of combination rules (any, all, at
least k positive), comparisons between tests, structured models for
conditional dependence, non-ignorable verification and explicit
sensitivity analyses for quantities that the data cannot identify.
Models are written in 'Stan' and can be fitted with the 'cmdstanr' or
'rstan' interfaces.
